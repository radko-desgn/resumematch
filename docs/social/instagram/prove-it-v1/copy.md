## Angle
Reframe the discouragement of repeated rejection: it's usually not that you're
underqualified, it's that your CV doesn't *prove* what the role needs. That leads
straight into the requirement→evidence feature. Distinct from the four existing
posts (fit, ethics, score-vs-evidence, coming-soon) by leading with the
candidate's self-doubt rather than the CV or the score.

## Caption (~130 words, humanized)
Getting rejected over and over does something to your head. You start assuming
you're just not good enough for the roles you actually want.

Usually that's not it. You can do the job. Your CV just doesn't prove it for this
specific role. Recruiters and filters go off what's written down, the evidence,
not your potential. So a capable person with a vague CV loses to a less capable
one who spelled it out.

ResumeMatch checks your CV against the exact job before you apply. It ties every
requirement to real evidence in your CV and shows what's met, what's partial, and
what's missing. Nothing invented, just what's already there, made clear.

Launching soon. Follow along so you catch it the day it's live.
