# ResumeMatch Content Calendar Planner

Plan a balanced schedule, not a pile of sales posts. Read **`resumematch-social`**
first.

## Template E — inputs
campaign length · posting frequency · launch date (only if confirmed; else leave
as placeholder) · available assets · primary conversion goal.

## Output
```yaml
calendar:
  - seq:            # or date if known
    pillar:
    funnel_stage:
    format:
    concept:
    hook:
    proof_asset:    # what's needed; mark [TO CREATE] / [EXISTS]
    cta:
    effort:         # low | med | high
    status:         # idea | drafted | rendered | scheduled
    reuse:          # can it be repurposed to a reel/single/story?
content_balance:    # counts per pillar + a one-line check
dependencies:       # assets/screens that gate posts
production_queue:    # ordered by effort/dependency
measurement_plan:    # what to track per post; weekly review note
```

## Balance rules
- Education + pain lead; product posts demonstrate, not announce.
- Founder posts use repo-true facts only.
- Launch posts are rare; never two direct-sales posts back to back.
- Every CTA must be currently supported (default "Follow for launch").
- Mark any launch date as a placeholder unless the founder confirmed it.
